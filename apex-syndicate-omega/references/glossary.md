# Glossary (plain words for the user)

Use these meanings when a term has to be explained. Say it in the user's language, in one short sentence, the first time it appears.

- **Document ID (D-01, D-02...)**: the number given to each paper you uploaded, so every finding can point to the exact paper and page.
- **Lineage ID (L-01...)**: papers that were copied or made from the same original share one lineage. Two papers from one original do not prove each other.
- **T1 to T4 (four evidence results)**: T1 did I read it correctly; T2 does the paper agree with itself; T3 does a paper from a different original support it; T4 has someone other than me confirmed it is genuine and complete. T4 stays NOT VERIFIED until a certified copy, an original, an audit trail or an expert confirms it.
- **Critical Value Ledger**: a short list of the dates, times, lab values, doses and amounts that a finding depends on, each re-read from the source page.
- **Benchmark Record**: the written medical guideline or standard we measure the care against, with its date, and why it fits this patient.
- **Deviation, harm, causation, counterfactual**: four separate questions. Was the care different from what a reasonable doctor would do; was there harm; did that difference cause the harm; would the outcome probably have been better with proper care.
- **Proceeding Register / Forum status**: the list of cases or complaints, and whether the forum (court or commission) is ESTABLISHED by a paper, PRESUMED as a working guess, or UNKNOWN.
- **PRESUMED-FORUM**: a date or rule worked out on a guessed forum. Do not rely on it until the forum is established.
- **Deadline Register**: the list of dates by which something must be done, with the rule used and how it was counted. An advocate must confirm every date.
- **VERIFIED / NOT VERIFIED**: VERIFIED means the official text or judgment was opened and matched. NOT VERIFIED means it was not, so the advocate must check it before it is relied on.
- **Disconfirmation**: deliberately looking for evidence that goes against our own finding before we keep it.
- **Red Team**: a hard attack on our own case, the way the hospital's lawyer would attack it.
- **Self-goal**: something the hospital's own paper says that helps us, quoted in context.
- **Traceability Map**: a table showing which fact and which paper stands behind each sentence of a draft.
- **Citation Registry**: the list of every law and case relied on, with whether it was verified.
- **Output Integrity Gate**: a line-by-line consistency check of a draft (numbers, references, annexures, dates, amounts). It tests consistency, not whether the facts or law are true.
- **Completion Gate**: the short note at the end of each part saying what is complete and what is not.
- **Draft status line**: WORKING DRAFT means checks have not been run or have failed. DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW means the checks ran with flags, or a critical item is still NOT VERIFIED. GATE PASSED means the consistency checks passed and everything cited was verified, and an enrolled advocate must still review it.
- **ADVOCATE COPY / CLEAN COPY**: the advocate copy keeps two kinds of bracketed notes (citations to verify, blanks to fill). The clean copy is made only after the advocate has cleared every bracket.
- **Change Register**: a list of every change made to your draft and why.

# Glossary (plain words for the user)

Use these meanings when a term has to be explained. Say it in the user's language, in one short sentence, the first time it appears.

- **Document ID (D-01, D-02...)**: the number given to each paper you uploaded, so every finding can point to the exact paper and page.
- **Lineage ID (L-01...)**: papers that were copied or made from the same original share one lineage. Two papers from one original do not prove each other.
- **T1 to T4 (four evidence results)**: T1 did I read it correctly; T2 does the paper agree with itself; T3 does a paper from a different original support it; T4 has someone other than me confirmed it is genuine and complete. T4 stays NOT VERIFIED until a certified copy, an original, an audit trail or an expert confirms it.
- **Critical Value Ledger**: a short list of the dates, times, lab values, doses and amounts that a finding depends on, each re-read from the source page.
- **Benchmark Record**: the written medical guideline or standard we measure the care against, with its date, and why it fits this patient.
- **Deviation, harm, causation, counterfactual**: four separate questions. Was the care different from what a reasonable doctor would do; was there harm; did that difference cause the harm; would the outcome probably have been better with proper care.
- **Proceeding Register / Forum status**: the list of cases or complaints, and whether the forum (court or commission) is ESTABLISHED by a paper, PRESUMED as a working guess, or UNKNOWN.
- **PRESUMED-FORUM**: a date or rule worked out on a guessed forum. Do not rely on it until the forum is established.
- **Deadline Register**: the list of dates by which something must be done, with the rule used and how it was counted. An advocate must confirm every date.
- **VERIFIED / NOT VERIFIED**: VERIFIED means the official text or judgment was opened and matched. NOT VERIFIED means it was not, so the advocate must check it before it is relied on.
- **Disconfirmation**: deliberately looking for evidence that goes against our own finding before we keep it.
- **Red Team**: a hard attack on our own case, the way the hospital's lawyer would attack it.
- **Self-goal**: something the hospital's own paper says that helps us, quoted in context.
- **Traceability Map**: a table showing which fact and which paper stands behind each sentence of the Vakil File.
- **Citation Registry**: the list of every law and case relied on, with whether it was verified.
- **File Integrity Gate**: a line-by-line consistency check of the Vakil File (point count, sources, references, dates, amounts). It tests consistency, not whether the facts or law are true.
- **Completion Gate**: the short note at the end of each part saying what is complete and what is not.
- **File status line**: WORKING FILE means the checks have not been run yet. FILE CHECKED WITH FLAGS means the checks ran and some items still need the advocate (for example a law that is NOT VERIFIED). FILE CHECKED means every fact is traced to its paper and every law shows its status. None of these means "ready to file": the advocate prepares and files the court papers.
- **Vakil File**: the one file this skill makes for your advocate: the big points that can win the case, each with its proof and page, the hospital's reply, our answer, the law and the final argument, plus our weak points and how to cure them. It is not a document to file in court.
- **Change Register**: when you give your own draft, the list of problems found in it and the suggested fixes, for your advocate to decide.
- **TAGDA / power line**: TAGDA means making a point as strong as the evidence allows, not making it shorter. A power line is one finding written with its fact, its source, how it stands against the record, the law and what it means for the case.
- **Source labels (SROT-SAMARTHIT TATHYA, SROT-SAAR, INFERENCE, LEGAL PROPOSITION, UNRESOLVED)**: how far a statement can be trusted. SROT-SAMARTHIT TATHYA: read in the original paper, with page. SROT-SAAR: taken from a summary or someone's account, not yet checked in the original. INFERENCE: our reasoning from facts. LEGAL PROPOSITION: a statement of law, with whether it was verified. UNRESOLVED: the source is missing or two sources disagree.
- **Source tier (TIER-A, TIER-B, TIER-C)**: which paper to trust first for each kind of statement. TIER-A is the original (the order itself, the original Written Statement, the hospital's own record page); TIER-C is only a lead (a summary or memory). Not the same as T1 to T4.
- **RECORD GAP / NOT READ**: RECORD GAP means the pages we read have no entries for a period; it does not mean nothing was done. NOT READ means we have not read that page yet, so we say nothing about what is or is not on it.
- **WS Location**: the page and paragraph of the hospital's original written reply (Written Statement) where its words were read. "MOOL WS NAHI DEKHA" means the original reply was not seen.
- **Version A / Version B**: two papers giving a different date, amount or order of events. Both are written down with their sources; one is called correct only when the original settles it.
- **ELEMENT TAG (Duty, Breach, Causation, Damage)**: the four things a negligence case must show: there was a duty of care, it was broken, that break caused the harm, and the harm and its amount. Procedural or Maintainability marks a point about the forum, limitation or the case being properly filed.
- **Evidence ID (EV-001...)**: the number given to one exact piece of proof (a page, a paragraph, an entry), so every argument points to the same place.
- **Master Reference File**: the formal name of the Vakil File: a point-by-point guide for the advocate, in simple Hinglish, that an ordinary reader can also follow: our point, the hospital's reply, our answer, the proof and law, and the final argument.
- **Audit Trail Table**: the table at the end of a final text listing what is verified, what is not yet checked, which papers are missing, what is still disputed, which laws need checking, and the questions for the advocate.
- **Correction Register**: the list of mistakes found in our own earlier work, what was corrected, and what had to be re-checked because of it.
- **DOCUMENTED / AS RECORDED / ALLEGED / INFERRED**: how an event in the timeline stands. DOCUMENTED: the record shows it. AS RECORDED: the entry is there, but whether it is true, or was done at that time, is not shown. ALLEGED: one side says so, no paper supports it yet. INFERRED: our reasoning, with its basis.
- **CASE IMPACT**: the block at the top of the reply after you send a new paper, saying what that paper makes stronger, what it makes weaker, which open points it settles, and what to do next.
- **Missing-record steps**: when the hospital does not give a record: a written request with proof, a reminder, an application to the Commission, and only after a refusal an argument that the Commission may hold the missing record against the hospital.
